Fantasy Sidekick

SMS checklists and reminders for fantasy football beginners.

Fantasy Sidekick sends timely text reminders about lineups, waivers, and free agency. To opt in, text START or JOIN to the Fantasy Sidekick phone number. Message frequency is typically up to 5 messages per week during the NFL season. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out; reply HELP for help.
