Privacy Policy

Fantasy Sidekick · Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Fantasy Sidekick (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our SMS reminder service for fantasy football.

Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Mobile phone numbers you provide so we can send SMS reminders
  • Opt-in / opt-out preferences and related messaging consent records
  • Basic delivery metadata from our messaging provider (for example, message status)
  • League-related information you connect for personalization (when enabled), such as public fantasy league identifiers

How we use information

We use this information only to:

  • Send fantasy football checklists and reminder SMS messages you opted into
  • Honor STOP / HELP and other carrier-required messaging commands
  • Operate, troubleshoot, and improve the service

SMS / mobile information — non-sharing statement

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell personal information.

We may use service providers strictly necessary to deliver messages (for example, Twilio as our SMS delivery provider). Those providers process data only to provide messaging services on our behalf and are not authorized to use your mobile information for their own marketing.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies. You may receive up to 5 messages per week during the NFL season (fewer in the offseason). Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP to any message to cancel. Reply HELP for help. You may also contact us using the details below to request removal.

Data retention

We retain phone numbers and consent records only as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal or carrier compliance obligations. After you opt out, we keep only what is required to honor the opt-out and prevent unwanted messages.
